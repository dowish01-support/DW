Request Account Deletion

Last Updated: March 30, 2026

At DoWish, we value your privacy. If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated personal data, please follow the instructions below.

Steps to Delete Your Account

  1. Send an email to dowishapps@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: "Delete My DoWish Account".
  3. Include the email address associated with your DoWish account in the body of the message.

Our team will verify your identity and process your request within 30 days.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Profile Data: Your name, email address, and profile settings.
  • Social Connections: Your friend list and pending requests.
  • Subscription History: Records of your premium status (Note: Active subscriptions must be cancelled via the Google Play Store or Apple App Store).

Data That May Be Retained

To maintain the experience for other users, Wishes or messages shared in communal spaces may remain visible but will be permanently anonymized. Your personal identity will be removed from this content. Financial or tax-related data may be retained for up to 7 years as required by law.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once completed, you will lose access to all premium features and your data cannot be recovered.
